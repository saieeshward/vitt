# Trademark policy

The Apache License 2.0 covering this source code grants **no rights to the
project's name, logo, or icon** (see LICENSE §6). Those are handled here.

## What you may do

- Fork, modify, and redistribute the source code under Apache-2.0.
- State truthfully that your work is "based on VITT" or "a fork of VITT."
- Use the name in commentary, tutorials, reviews, and academic work.

## What you may not do

- **Publish a build to any app store under the name "VITT"**, or under any
  name or icon likely to be confused with it. This includes Google Play, the
  Apple App Store, and third-party Android repositories.
- Use the VITT name, logo, or icon as the identity of your fork or of any
  product or service.
- Imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, the VITT project.

## Why

The code is open so you can read it, audit it, learn from it, and run your own
build. The name exists so users can tell which build is the one the maintainers
publish and support. Store policy — Apple 4.1 (Copycats), 4.3 (Spam), 5.2.1 and
Google Play's Impersonation and Spam policies — is enforced through trademark,
not through copyright, which is why this document exists alongside a permissive
licence.

## Renaming a fork

Pick a name and icon that are not confusable with VITT's, change the
`applicationId` / bundle identifier, and use your own signing keys. Keep the
Apache-2.0 LICENSE and NOTICE files intact, as the licence requires.

Questions: open an issue.
