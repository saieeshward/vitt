Expense tracking for more than one currency

Money in more than one currency, kept apart.

VITT is an expense tracker for people whose money lives in euro, rupees and dollars at once. It never converts one into another.

Follow on GitHub See how it works

One stack each

Every currency keeps its own balance.

Coins go into their own stack and never move between them. No total changes because an exchange rate did, because there is no total to change.

The one exception

The only rate it records is one you used.

When you move money across a border, the rate that transfer used is written down as a fact. Nothing else is ever converted.

How it works

Built for the thirty seconds after you pay.

Most people stop logging because it takes too long, or because they would rather not see the number. VITT is built around both.

01

Two taps to log.

Type the amount and press Save. The account is the one you used last and everything else is optional. Something you log often, like the same coffee at the same price, waits at the top, so a repeat is one tap and Save.

02

One card per currency.

Ledgers shows each currency on its own card, with its budget and what came in. Accounts sit underneath, grouped by currency and never summed across them.

03

Every entry, day by day.

Activity lists everything by day. Categories come from the merchant, and when VITT is unsure it asks rather than guesses. Correct one and it remembers that shop.

04

Sorting takes one pass.

Entries without a category come up one at a time, with every category beside them. A tap files the entry and shows the next, and the same shop's older entries go with it.

05

Splits that add up.

A bill is shared equally until you type somebody's part, and a part you typed stays where you put it, the way Tricount works. The rest is split between whoever you did not type. When someone pays you back, it is one tap on their name.

06

The month, told back.

Once a month has enough in it, Reports offers it back as a short story: the days you logged, where most of it went, the quiet days. Facts about the month, never a verdict about you.

Your data

A spreadsheet you own. No account, no server.

Everything lives in a database on your phone and, if you connect Google, in a sheet VITT made in your own Drive. The app only ever adds rows, so two phones or a week offline merge without losing anything. Stop using VITT and every row is still yours.

What it will not do

The things it leaves alone are the point.

It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

The only Google permission it asks for is drive.file, which covers the one sheet VITT made or a file you pick. Nothing else in your Drive is visible to it.

It does not read your texts.

No SMS, no notifications, no bank login. You log an entry, share one in, or import a CSV from your bank. Nothing is scraped behind your back.

There is no VITT account.

There is nothing to sign up for and no VITT server holding your spending. Connecting Google is optional, and the app works fully without it.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking.

No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is in the app, and it never converts your money into one blended number to show you.

The companion

Someone to keep you company.

A small animal walks along the foot of the app and carries one marker per currency. It can be curious about an entry waiting for a category. It is never disappointed in you. Pick one in Settings, or turn it off.
